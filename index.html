02 / THE QUESTIONFROM APPEARANCE TO ATTRIBUTION

Seeing an object.
Knowing its story.

A vase made in America can look Dutch. Recognising a form does not tell us who made it, where, or when.

Pothen asks a vision-language model to infer five catalogue fields from an image alone. We compare its predictions with curator-written museum records, and ask whether the fields agree on the same object.

Five plausible fields rarely make one complete attribution.

Across 6,542 valid outputs, only 393 records have all five fields compatible with the museum reference. Mean field compatibility is 44.01%; compatibility across all four contextual fields is 13.30%.

Evaluation uses the stored full-run DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash judge (DS-J), calibrated against human annotations. Scores measure catalogue compatibility under the released rubric. Empty references assess uncertainty handling; 208 invalid outputs are reported separately.
